Columns

One row per column, in display order — drag a row by its handle (or use ▲▼) to reorder, calculated rows included. Rows may follow reference hops to parent or child entities (any depth) and end on one of their attributes; leave empty to show every attribute. Numeric columns can carry a grand total (sum / average / min / max) over the view's filtered rows.

A calculated row derives its value per record: Text joins the display value of two or more rows, with an optional separator; Number evaluates an arithmetic formula over numeric rows — + - * /, parentheses, unary minus and round(value, digits) — referencing a column by its key in braces, e.g. round({price} * {quantity}, 2). An empty result shows the em dash.
